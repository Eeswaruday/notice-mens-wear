Frequently Asked Questions

Are all products authentic?

Yes, 100% authentic. We only stock genuine products from authorized distributors. Every product carries the brand's authenticity guarantee.

What if the size doesn't fit?

We have a flexible exchange policy. If the fit isn't perfect, bring the product back and we'll exchange it for the right size at no additional cost.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes! We offer home delivery for orders within Ongole and surrounding areas. Cash on Delivery available for your convenience.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, card payments, and digital payments. For online orders, UPI, net banking, and credit/debit cards are available.

How do I find the right size?

Use our size guide above or visit us in-store. Our expert staff can take measurements and recommend the perfect size for you.

When does your store open?

We're open daily. We close at 10:00 PM. For exact opening time, call us at 099856 93995.
